📖 Wisdom Playbook for Work

A comprehensive, critical, practical guide for surviving, growing, leading, and staying sane at work.

Work wisdom is not just "working hard," "being smart," or "being nice." It is the ability to understand people, incentives, timing, power, quality, communication, politics, and your own limits — then act with maturity.

⚡ Wisdom = Competence + Judgment + Communication + Character + Timing
The Core Truth
The wisest people at work are not the loudest or most talented. They know what matters, who matters, when to speak, when to wait, when to push, and when to grow up.
3 Layers
Task Wisdom · People Wisdom · System Wisdom — you need all three to navigate any workplace.
10 Commandments
From "Activity ≠ Value" to "Know When To Leave" — the foundational laws of work wisdom.
